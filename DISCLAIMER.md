- Partner titles and likenesses remain subject to their original rights holders.

### Before making this repo public

1. Review every PDF/XLS under [`receipts/contracts/`](./receipts/contracts) — redact addresses, phone numbers, tax IDs, bank details, or anything you do not want public.
2. Review [`receipts/royalty/`](./receipts/royalty) the same way (or replace with a redacted PDF/PNG export).
3. Confirm you are comfortable publishing partner promo stills under [`receipts/partner-promo/`](./receipts/partner-promo).
4. Remove or replace any file you are unsure about.
